# Mystical Isles

A player-shaped MMORPG set across a fractured archipelago where exploration, danger, and faction ambition redefine the map every season.

## 🌊 What Is Mystical Isles?
Mystical Isles is a cooperative online RPG focused on island exploration, combat expeditions, crafting economies, and territorial identity. Players start small, survive the frontier, and gradually influence trade routes, settlements, and political power.

## ⚔ Core Gameplay Pillars
- Exploration & discovery
- Tactical combat
- Gathering & crafting
- Trade & economy
- Building & settlement growth
- Faction reputation
- Naval travel
- Cooperative progression

## 🏝 The World
The archipelago was shattered by an ancient catastrophe called **The Fracturing**. Today, the Mainland Kingdom, Witchwood, Frostpeak, Ashen Deadlands, Shattered Reefs, and Stormreach all compete for control of relics, routes, and survival.

## 🧱 Player-Driven World
Players shape the world by:
- founding and upgrading claims
- controlling resource loops
- building faction standing
- opening or destabilizing travel routes
- driving regional trade and scarcity

## 🛠 Technology Stack
- Unity
- Atavism X 9
- SYNTY POLYGON packs
- Gaia + GeNa
- World Streamer
- Enviro + supporting middleware

## 🚧 Current Development Phase
Current focus is a **Minimum Playable MMORPG**: one playable island, one city, one dungeon, basic combat/progression, foundational crafting, merchants, and exploration loops.

## 📜 Developer Vision
Mystical Isles is designed as a long-term social sandbox where systems are interdependent and player stories become persistent world history.
